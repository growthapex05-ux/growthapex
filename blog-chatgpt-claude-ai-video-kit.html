2. Phase 1: High-Retention Scriptwriting with Claude 3.7

Retaining audience attention past the critical 3-second threshold requires a psychological pattern interrupt. Claude 3.7 Sonnet excels at analyzing trending viral hook structures and generating script blueprints designed to keep retention above 85%.

Master Prompt: The 3-Second Viral Retention Hook Formula

"Act as a master viral scriptwriter for YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Write a 45-second high-retention script on [Topic]. 
Structure the script into 4 exact sections:
1. THE PATTERN INTERRUPT (0-3s): A controversial or curiosity-driven hook.
2. THE PROBLEM STAKING (3-12s): Amplify the viewer's pain point or fascination.
3. THE VALUE DELIVERY (12-35s): 3 rapid-fire actionable takeaways with precise visual scene descriptors.
4. THE INFINITE LOOP CALL TO ACTION (35-45s): End with a sentence that seamlessly loops back to line 1."
